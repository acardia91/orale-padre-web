Who we are

Three locations,
one kitchen

Órale Padre is Mexican street food made in Seville: burritos, tacos, nachos and bowls. It was born in 2021 as a dark kitchen in Los Remedios, in the former Puglia pizza place, where much of the team comes from. Today there are three locations with a central kitchen where everything is prepared. The cochinita and the pork cheek cook low and slow for hours, and the tortilla is heated when you order, not when we open. That's why we take fifteen minutes and not three.

01

Low and slow

The cochinita and the pork cheek cook low and slow for hours. There's no shortcut, so we don't take one.

02

Made today

Guacamole, pico de gallo and salsas are made every morning, just enough for the day. We never serve you yesterday's.

03

By hand

Every burrito is rolled one by one when you order. Fifteen minutes, not three, and you can tell.

Quick questions

What people
always ask

Do you deliver in Seville?

Yes, via Uber Eats and Glovo from all three locations. And if you pick it up yourself, order on Last App with no fees and it's ready in 15–25 minutes.

How much is a burrito?

€9.95 for almost all of them. The Doña Juana, the plant-based one, is €12.50. Combos with chips and a drink from €14.95, and the Thursday and Friday lunch menu is €10.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

The Doña Juana, with Heura instead of meat, is 100% plant-based. Any bowl can be ordered dairy-free. If you're strictly vegan, ask us about the tortilla before ordering.

Can I book a table?

Yes, right here or by calling the restaurant. For groups over ten, write to us and we'll tailor it.

Where are you?

At Calle San Luis 78 (Macarena), Virgen de Loreto 16 (Los Remedios) and Calle Japón 10 (Sevilla Este). All three with the griddle in plain view.

Do you have allergen information?

On every dish in the menu, by pressing the +. Everything is cooked in the same kitchen, so if you have an allergy tell us when ordering and we'll say what's safe for you.
