Cookies

This site uses no own or third-party cookies for analytics or advertising, which is why you won't see a cookie banner.

  • We store your chosen location in your browser (localStorage) so we don't ask every time. It's technical data, identifies no one and never leaves your device. It's deleted by clearing the site data in your browser.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the files from Google's servers (and shares your IP address with them). Google receives no other information about you from this site.
  • If we add analytics in the future, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent first.

My Órale Padre club terms

These terms govern the My Órale Padre loyalty programme. Signing up means accepting them. The plain-language version is on the club page; if in doubt, this text prevails.

  • Who can join. Over 18s. One account per person and per phone number. Signing up is free and requires no purchase.
  • The two welcome tacos. When you sign up, we treat you to two tacos with your first pick-up order at any of the three locations, by showing the welcome message or identifying yourself with the phone number you signed up with. One redemption per person, not exchangeable for cash or another product, with a minimum order of one main dish. Valid for 60 days from sign-up.
  • How points add up. One euro spent equals one point on in-store orders (table, bar or pick-up, identifying yourself with your phone number when paying) and two points on pick-up orders placed through the website or our online shop. Orders through delivery platforms (Uber Eats, Glovo) earn no points because the platform doesn't tell us who the customer is. The Compadre and Padrino levels multiply points as shown on the club page.
  • Redeeming. With 100 points you can redeem a burrito from the menu (except Doña Juana and special editions) on any in-store or pick-up order. Redeeming deducts 100 points. Points cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Expiry. Points expire 12 months after the last order that earned points. Your level is worked out from the last 12 months of orders.
  • Birthday. If you give us your date, you get a free dessert with any in-store order during your birthday week, once a year.
  • Changes and end of the programme. We may change these terms or close the programme with 30 days' notice by email and on this page. Points already earned can be redeemed during that period.
  • Misuse. We reserve the right to cancel accounts or points obtained with false data, duplicate accounts or any fraudulent use.
  • Leaving. You can leave whenever you like by writing to orale.padre.food@gmail.com. Leaving deletes your points.

Allergens

Under Regulation (EU) 1169/2011, every dish on the menu lists the allergens it contains: press the + on any dish to see them. This is the list of the 14 allergens that must be declared:

  • 1 Cereals containing gluten
  • 2 Crustaceans
  • 3 Eggs
  • 4 Fish
  • 5 Peanuts
  • 6 Soya
  • 7 Milk and dairy
  • 8 Tree nuts
  • 9 Celery
  • 10 Mustard
  • 11 Sesame
  • 12 Sulphites
  • 13 Lupin
  • 14 Molluscs

Important: everything is cooked in the same kitchen, so we can't guarantee the total absence of traces of any allergen. If you have an allergy or intolerance, tell the team before ordering, in store or by phone: we'll tell you which dishes are safe for you and what we can adapt. The nutritional values shown for the bowls are estimates calculated from the recipe, not lab analyses.
